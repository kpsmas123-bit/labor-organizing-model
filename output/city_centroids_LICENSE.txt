Source file used: cities1000.zip (places with population >= 1,000)
Filtered to: United States only (country_code = US), top 5,000 by population,
             excluding sub-city sections (feature_code PPLX/PPLCH/PPLQ/PPLW).
Fields retained: city name, state abbreviation (admin1_code), latitude, longitude.
Population field used for sorting only; not included in output.
