How Intervention Types Work
Type A
Build New Power
Few or no unions here. High potential for new organizing. Start from scratch — find the organic leaders, map the workplace, build the structure.
Low infrastructure, high opportunity
Type B
Wake the Giant
Unions exist but aren't punching their weight politically. The organized power is there — it needs activation through political action and internal leadership development.
Moderate–high infrastructure, low political alignment
Type C
Move Together
Strong unions, politically engaged. Best terrain for coordinated campaigns and electoral leverage. Build on existing infrastructure to maximize impact.
High infrastructure, high alignment
