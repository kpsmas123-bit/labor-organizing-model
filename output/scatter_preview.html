Methods & findings

Data plotted

Axes

X = Electoral Leverage = national p1_national (the electoral-strategic value), plotted on a fixed 0–100 scale.

Y = Sectoral Leverage = combine( normalize(sls_capital)→0–100 , normalize(sls_community)→0–100 ). Each component is min-max normalized so its max across all counties = 100. The two are combined per the labor_score_combination rule.

Thresholds & quadrant lines — values used

Color — Incumbent Alignment buckets

Model sanity check — do the 6 tiers line up with the quadrants?

What the five toggles do

  • Combine rule — recomputes Y live as max ↔ average of the two normalized components, so you can compare the Y-axis definition without editing config.
  • Color by — Incumbent Alignment (P2) ↔ 6-tier. Tier coloring is the geometry↔classification sanity check.
  • Alignment filter — show all ↔ hostile-only (P2 < ceiling) ↔ aligned-only (P2 ≥ floor).
  • Quadrant lines — toggle the divider lines + quadrant labels on/off.
  • Overplot — point-opacity slider + jitter slider, so the dense bottom-left Low-Priority blob stays readable.
