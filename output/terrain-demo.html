Data methodology

How we score organizing terrain

Each county receives a composite score drawn from BLS occupational data, NLRB election records, Census demographic indicators, and state-level policy environment ratings. Scores are normalized 0–100 and updated annually.

About this tool

Built for organizers, not just researchers

Most labor data lives in spreadsheets and policy reports. Terrain puts it on a map — filterable by industry, score tier, and geography — so you can move from data to strategy in a single session.
